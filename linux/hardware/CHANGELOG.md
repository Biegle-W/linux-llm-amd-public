# Changelog

Newest first. One line per change, with a link to the dated report holding its evidence. Current state lives in the [runtime handoff](../network/LINUX-LLM-RUNTIME-HANDOFF.md), not here. Engine updates, manual since November 9, have their own [ledger](DAILY-UPDATES.md).

## November 2026

- **Nov 11, repository audit and cleanup.** Stale wording, dead links and two failing unit tests fixed across 18 files; nothing was deleted and no runtime file changed. Open owner decisions are listed in the report: the launchers export GPU order `1,0` while `rocm-env.sh` documents `0,1`, the public mirror's engine references are out of date, and about 23 GB of superseded build and cache folders remain. [Report](REPO-AUDIT-2026-11-11.md)
- **Nov 11, docs aligned with manual engine updates.** The top README, this file's header and the update ledger still described a scheduled nightly updater. They now say what has been true since November 9: updates are checked, installed and qualified by hand, and no timer runs the old script. The README route count is corrected to nine. No engine, route or schedule changed. [Ledger](DAILY-UPDATES.md)
- **Nov 11, DeepSeek Strata replay stopped on NaN again.** The 216-item public suite was replayed on `deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-strata-unsloth-q1` at the 2 GiB reserve: 88 items attempted, 5 NaN errors (two isolated, then three in a row with no tokens generated), run aborted by the harness. No kernel GPU messages and no hold. 64 of the 82 answered items were correct against 66 for Maya on the same items. No setting changed. [Benchmark list](CURRENT-AMD-BENCHMARKS.md)
- **Nov 11, three routes removed (owner request).** `ornith-1.5-122b`, `sage-a1.5` and `flint-40b-a2.5b` are gone from the route list, llama.cpp and vLLM profiles, offload plans and client names, and the Sage launcher is deleted; nine routes remain. Tests that used Sage as a stand-in route now use `qwen3.8-27b-vllm`. No engine changed and the hub was not restarted. The three weight folders were deleted after owner confirmation (114 GB freed on sharedSATA1). They ranked 6, 8 and 11 of 11 on the public suite. [Benchmark list](CURRENT-AMD-BENCHMARKS.md)
- **Nov 11, quality ranking added to the current results.** The benchmark list now has a quality-rank column and a ranking table from the 216-item public suite: DeepSeek on Maya, Flash-Next and both Qwen3.8-27B routes tie for the top; Flint is last. No new measurement. [Table](CURRENT-AMD-BENCHMARKS.md#quality-ranking)
- **Nov 11, public-benchmark quality suite.** Eleven routes answered 216 items sampled from MMLU-Pro, MATH-500, AIME, BigCodeBench, IFEval, BFCL and MuSR. DeepSeek on Maya 168, Flash-Next 164, Qwen3.8-27B 163 (SGLang) and 157 (vLLM) lead and are not separated; Flint is lowest at 121; Ripple (134) had 65 answers cut off by the token limit. DeepSeek on Strata returned NaN errors and was not measured. No route or engine changed. [Report](PUBLIC-QUALITY-SUITE-2026-11-11.md)
- **Nov 10, Flint and Ripple quality rerun.** With every answer budget raised to at least 4,096 tokens, Flint scores 51/54 and 62/64 (was 43 and 36) and Ripple 53/54 and 63/64 (was 50 and 56), in line with the other routes. The November 9 scores were empty answers from the 60-token limit. No route or engine changed. [Report](FLINT-RIPPLE-BUDGET-RERUN-2026-11-10.md)
- **Nov 9, ComfyUI idle stop.** ComfyUI now follows the same 600 s idle rule as the hub's LLM engines: it stops itself after 10 minutes with an empty queue and no browser tab connected, which also ends its idle CPU cost (about 2 cores). Start it again with `graphics/start-comfyui` or the desktop icon. [Details](../graphics/H3-TP2-PRODUCTION-2026-11-07.md)
- **Nov 9, manual engine update.** llama.cpp `0a94a3f`, Strata `504b143` and Maya `0065548` (1.1.57) promoted after isolated builds, per-card parity and strict short DSH tool checks on all six of their routes. Maya tool round trips are much faster (DeepSeek 106 s vs 198 s, GLM 31 s vs 116 s). A Strata decode dip in the short check was a 76-token timing artifact; a 400-token A/B showed old and new equal (about 52 tok/s). `maya/runtime.json` pin reconciled. Strata DeepSeek `504b143` (0.1.59.1) followed the same evening: one marginal IQ3_XXS parity warning (as on November 7), tool check passed at unchanged speed, load 68 s vs 140 s; its stale runtime pin reconciled. [Ledger](DAILY-UPDATES.md)
- **Nov 9, scripted updater retired.** At the owner's request `llm-daily-update.timer` is disabled; engine updates are now checked and installed by hand. First check: llama.cpp, Strata and Maya have updates worth taking; vLLM and SGLang are current. [Ledger](DAILY-UPDATES.md)
- **Nov 9, all-models tool call and quality rerun.** All 12 routes passed the November 7 tool-call standard and finished both quality suites with no errors. Strongest: DeepSeek (54/54; 62–63/64). Flint and Ripple score low because they reason through the 60-token short-answer budget. Three stops, none GPU faults: `short_trial.py` still required 6 GiB free VRAM per card (now the owner policy), desktop GPU apps left card 0 below Maya's 1 GiB floor, and GLM's expert warm-up stalled the harness heartbeat on the system drive (rerun with a 30 s heartbeat, owner choice). [Report](AMD-ALL-MODELS-TOOL-AND-QUALITY-2026-11-09.md)
- **Nov 9, legacy memory guard removed.** The `LOCAL_LLM_LEGACY_RESOURCE_VALIDATION=1` replay of the pre-November 3 guard values is gone from `dsh/` and `hardware/swap_activity.py`; the owner policy (1 GiB RAM, 128 MiB VRAM per card) is the only path. 8 replay-only tests deleted, the rest converted; 652 tests pass (2 skipped). Three refusal messages corrected to the real thresholds. Also rebuilt the two-card peer integrity probe as `hardware/p2p-probe/peer_integrity.cpp` (the November 7 binary was lost in the November 8 cleanup).

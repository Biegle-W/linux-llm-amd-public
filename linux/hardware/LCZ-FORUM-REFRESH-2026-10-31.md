## New H3 repository lead

The [happymy plan](https://github.com/happymy/MinimaxH3-7900xtx/blob/bf9bb5123ec0d4c7b08ad53410a61a14c1c32ca0/plan/ComfyUI_MiniMaxH3_AMD_7900XTX_Plan.md), pinned at `bf9bb5123ec0`, offers 4B/8B encoders plus a learned ClipProj mapping, staged unloading, CK attention and preview-cost measurements. Its custom preview path adds 65.1 seconds in the reported short test; its 1.11× upgrade comparison changes the whole stack, not Torch alone. These are Windows/single-card results, not our Linux TP2 measurements.

**Assessment:** a smaller projected encoder is a reasonable isolated memory/load-time candidate, but it changes conditioning architecture. Matching vision-tower dimensions does not establish equal text, image-conditioned or audio-conditioned output quality. The plan's broad claim that the native 32B route is unusable on AMD is superseded locally by our corrected NVFP4 and native-HIP W4A8 results. CK attention and staged encoder/denoiser execution are already present in our tested path. Preview overhead is relevant only if the preview code actually runs; no corresponding local saving is established.

Our [matched encoder-format comparison](../graphics/H3-AMD-ENCODER-FORMATS-2026-10-28.md) preserves the native 50-layer 32B architecture and measures **5.91–8.11× encoding speedup** with native HIP INT8 W4A8 versus corrected NVFP4. That is an encoding-stage result, not a whole-video ratio. The 8B proposal would require a new matched semantic/media comparison, including reference-image behavior, loading, owned RAM, swap and full-request timing. No encoder was replaced or downloaded here.

The official [H3 prompt guide](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/docs/blob/main/tutorials/video/minimax/minimax-h3-prompt-guide.mdx) supplies an immediately usable workflow practice: structured scene/sound/music fields, positive descriptions, and explicit audio intent. Its CFG1 BasicGuider path has no negative branch. This is guidance for new prompts, not evidence of a measured speedup or a change to any saved workflow.

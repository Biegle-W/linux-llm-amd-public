# Broad quality ranking test on all ten routes — November 25–26, 2026

**Owner, November 25–26:** "a quality test that covers ranking in a broad mix: knowledge, math, code, following instructions, reasoning and some tool calls, that can be finished in 15 mins for smaller models and 30 mins for large models", then "Run the ranking test on all ten routes".

**Result:** all ten routes finished the 30-item core inside their budget, and all ten reached item 45, so the 45-item ranking below compares every route on the same items. Flash-Next (37 of 45), Qwen3.8-27B on SGLang (36) and DeepSeek on Maya (35) lead; GLM (22) and Inkling (23) are last. Only four pairs are separated at 45 items: Flash-Next and SGLang above Inkling, SGLang and DeepSeek on Maya above GLM. Among the seven routes that reached 105 items, Flash-Next (89 of 105) is separated from Hy4-Flash, Kolibri, GLM and Inkling, and SGLang (80) from Inkling. With the format-tolerant re-grade below, Flash-Next and SGLang are no longer separated from Inkling at 45 items.

## What was run

- **Test:** the [rank tier](quant-eval/rank/README.md) ([design](quant-eval/rank/DESIGN.md)), built by the agent council on November 25. A frozen list of 144 items from the public suite (MMLU-Pro, MATH-500 and AIME, BigCodeBench, IFEval, MuSR, BFCL single turn); the first 30 are the core every route must finish. After the core, each route keeps answering in the same order while the next item fits its budget: 15 minutes for the small routes, 30 for the large ones. Load, one warm-up request and grading are outside the clock.
- **Settings:** through the hub, one request at a time, temperature 0, thinking off, the public suite's answer limits (2,048 tokens; 3,072 for math and code; 4,096 for AIME). An answer cut off by the limit counts as wrong. Code runs in the `bwrap` sandbox with no network.
- **Engines:** as on November 25 after the daily update: Strata 0.2.22 (Flash-Next with the greedy default restored, two-card Inkling, Hy4-Flash), Maya 1.1.81 (GLM, DeepSeek), Strata DeepSeek `13eedf55`, vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp pull-request build (Kolibri).
- **Window:** 23:37 November 25 to 03:42 November 26 (SGLang ran first, on November 25), one route after another. No GPU hold, no kernel GPU error. The chain paused once after GLM, whose last request was still finishing on the hub when its clock ran out; it was restarted for the last three routes after the request ended.

## Answers the frozen grader could not read

The grader takes the text after the last `ANSWER:` marker and needs exactly one letter or one number. A read-only audit of every unreadable ("malformed") answer found two formatting causes that do not reflect the answer itself:

- **Bold final line.** Hy4-Flash (9 answers), Kolibri (5) and DeepSeek on Strata (1) sometimes write the last line as `**ANSWER: 40**`, so the payload ends in `**`. In all 15 the value inside the marker is the right answer.
- **Inkling on Strata.** In 18 of its 20 unreadable answers the reply holds two answer markers run together with no space between them, while the model's reasoning arrives in the separate reasoning field even though the request turns thinking off (on 11 of the 20 the reasoning stops at exactly 1,040 tokens). In 15 of those the second marker is right. The older Q8 weights on llama.cpp answered the same 20 items with none unreadable. This points at how Strata's Inkling engine assembles the reply, not at the model; the cause was not traced in the engine code.

The **format-tolerant** column re-grades only the answers marked unreadable, after removing the bold marks and splitting run-together markers; no correct or wrong answer is changed, and the grader itself is not changed. At 45 items it lifts Inkling from 23 to 27 (still ninth), Hy4-Flash from 28 to 29 and DeepSeek on Strata from 33 to 34, and removes two of the four separated pairs (Flash-Next and SGLang above Inkling; SGLang and DeepSeek on Maya above GLM remain); on the long checkpoints it lifts Inkling by up to 15 items and Hy4-Flash and Kolibri by up to 9 and 5. The official scores stay on the frozen grader, so they remain comparable with earlier runs.

## Limits

- At 45 items the 95% interval is about ±12 points, so routes a few items apart are not separated. Most of the order inside group A–D is not resolved.
- The deeper checkpoints include only the routes fast enough to reach them.
- One run per route at temperature 0. Run-to-run variation was not measured; the comparisons with November 10–11 show a few items flipping between runs on unchanged weights.
- Ripple reasons even with thinking off; 9 of its first 45 answers ran out of tokens while still reasoning and count as wrong, as in the public suite.
- The report note in `score_rank.py` still says timing is unknown without `run.json` even when it is supplied; wording only.

# Ornith-1.5-122B Q4 test — October 21, 2026

The user authorized downloading and testing Ornith after the
[assessment](ORNITH-122B-ASSESSMENT-2026-10-21.md). Codex downloaded and verified the files and ran
the first arms. Claude resumed the queue after an interruption. Ornith is a **test model** only:
it was not added to the active catalog, DSH default or launchers.

**Setup**
- Official Q4_K_M plus vision projector, with publisher checksums verified, on Shared SATA.
- llama.cpp `4c3c5810` on HIP with ROCm 7.2.3. Both RX 7900 XTX cards, layer split.
- 30 ordinary layers' experts in CPU RAM, plus a 4 GiB aggregate GPU expert cache.
- 32K context, q8_0 KV.
- Each arm passes arithmetic, constrained JSON, a forced tool round trip, SSE usage and
  a six-case code fixture. It then runs three fixed-seed timed trials per task: code decode,
  prose decode and a fresh ~8.2K native prefill.

**MTP gate (fixed before measuring):** keep MTP3 only if its geometric mean over
{code decode, prose decode, native prefill} beats no-MTP by more than 3%, with no phase more than
5% worse.

| Arm | Code decode t/s | Prose decode t/s | Native ~8.2K PP t/s | Fresh TTFT s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **MTP off** (r2) | **11.43** | **13.34** | **52.68** | 155.5 |
| MTP3 (r3) | 8.21 | 7.18 | 52.38 | 156.3 |
| MTP off, 12 batch threads + vision + 31K context | 11.41 | 13.32 | 52.67 | 155.5 |

MTP3 changed code, prose and prefill by −28.1%, −46.2% and −0.6%, a **−27.3% geometric
mean**. **MTP is rejected; Ornith keeps MTP off.** Raising batch threads from 6 to 12 made
no measurable difference.

**Context and vision (MTP off, 12 batch threads):**
- A fresh **30,988-token** input retrieved its marker. Time to first token was 594 s.
  Prefill speed is the limit at this size.
- The full-history follow-up (31,048 input, 31,021 cached) answered in 0.50 s TTFT.
- The synthetic image check passed: red square left, blue circle right.

**Memory**
- Every arm had zero worker swap and zero VRAM spill.
- Lowest free VRAM was 5.4 / 6.7 GiB, during the 31K arm.
- Lowest available host RAM was 10.6 GiB, during the MTP3 arm.
- The worker's 52 GiB cgroup cap reclaimed page cache repeatedly, with no OOM events.

**Thermals:** sampled junction peaks were 90 °C on GPU0 and 60 °C on GPU1, with VRAM at
84 / 72 °C and the CPU package at 85 °C. The upper card runs hotter, as in earlier tests.
SMU limit flags are recorded samples, not measured throttled time.

**Interruption and resume**
- Codex's first two launches stopped before loading the model. A copied helper asserted the
  wrong lock owner; no timing was affected.
- Codex's MTP3 r2 arm was interrupted at 12:30 during its functional stage, by a
  KeyboardInterrupt in the queue.
- Claude recorded the stale lock (its PID and unit were gone) and claimed `GPU-LOCK`.
  Claude then reran MTP3 as r3 and applied Codex's unchanged gate and follow-up arm.
- r2 (MTP off) and r3 (MTP3) ran sequentially, not interleaved. Three trials do not establish
  statistical significance, but the MTP3 decode loss far exceeds the gap between repeated
  no-MTP runs.

**Limits**
- No agent, SWE-bench or publisher-score reproduction.
- The near-window and code checks are smoke tests.
- Publisher 256K context is not locally validated; 32K is the tested window.

Evidence: [numeric record](results/ornith-122b-test-2026-10-21.json). Raw files are in ignored
`hardware/generated/ornith-122b-test-2026-10-21/`.

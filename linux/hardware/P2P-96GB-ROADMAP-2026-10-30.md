## Current topology and P2P

Read-only sysfs checks show the GPUs under separate CPU root ports, each with a Gen4 x8 upstream path. Their internal downstream links reporting x16 do not change that bottleneck. The retained [P2P diagnosis](PCIE-P2P-2026-10-17.md) records unsupported cross-root peer access.

Both GPUs should sit below the same transparent active switch. Local peer traffic can then remain within that switch; CPU transfers share its upstream link. The complete board's wiring, firmware, BAR/ACS behavior and HIP/RCCL peer capability must be verified. [Broadcom's product brief](https://docs.broadcom.com/doc/BC-0484EN) describes the switching/DMA capabilities; [AMD RCCL guidance](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/rccl/en/latest/how-to/rccl-usage-tips.html) requires capable GPUs and suitable large BAR addressing. Environment flags alone cannot establish a working route.

## What 96 GB changes

The saved [64 GB profile restoration rule](SSD-STREAMING-32GB-2026-10-28.md) requires at least 60 GiB visible physical RAM and idle inference. Restoring those exact profiles is the first step; larger 96 GB caches need new qualification. Expected beneficiaries include GLM/Maya, Ornith, DeepSeek XS and Qwen Flash Next/Strata. Larger caches should reduce paging/SSD misses; the size of any speed gain is unmeasured.

[Super INT4](SUPER-INT4-VLLM-PREFLIGHT-2026-10-29.md) was blocked before full-model startup by physical-RAM admission. Extra RAM makes that full qualification practical to attempt, not already passed. The [Strata RDNA3 trial](STRATA-RDNA3-32GB-TRIAL-2026-10-30.md) still has watchdog failures. More RAM may permit a layout without the current stage-trim restriction on DP prefill, subject to VRAM admission; it does not establish that the stalls are fixed. The 96 GB forum benchmark used host peer transport, so P2P is not a prerequisite for investigating its DP-prefill path.

## H3 target and order

Our [32 GB H3 comparison](../graphics/H3-MATCHED-32GB-2026-10-28.md) already completed real TP2 with host transport. P2P is an opportunity to improve communication; more RAM helps loading/conditioning/staging. A reasonable larger candidate starts with 1080p around 10 seconds, subject to all guards and assets being available. The forum's 12-second result used about 23 GiB per card, leaving roughly 1 GiB; our H3 hard free-VRAM floor is 1.5 GiB. That exact configuration therefore needs additional VRAM savings before qualification here. RAM and the switch do not increase per-card VRAM.

Recommended order: retain the completed offline source/readiness audit; validate topology and transport after the switch; verify capacity/stability and restore saved profiles after RAM installation; then qualify larger MoE and H3 candidates. Any driver/kernel activation or new GPU experiment remains a separately requested action. [Latest forum/source review](LCZ-UPDATE-REVIEW-2026-10-30.md).

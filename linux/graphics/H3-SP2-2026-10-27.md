The benchmark attempt is blocked; no completed matched generation comparison or speedup is available.
The active ComfyUI installation, saved workflows, weights and default selection
remain unchanged. Both AMD repositories retain the evidence, including failed
preparation, the stock loader failure and the isolated compatibility pilot.

## Memory policy retained

Every GPU attempt held `hardware/generated/GPU-LOCK` and the existing Comfy
server lock. Tests bind to loopback only. The existing graphics settings remain
`--reserve-vram 2 --disable-pinned-memory --disable-async-offload
--disable-dynamic-vram --cache-none --use-pytorch-cross-attention`.
The whole-card watchdog preserves 2 GiB per-card headroom (four low samples),
the 1.5 GiB hard stop, the 3 GiB available-RAM floor and strict zero owned-worker
swap / dedicated-VRAM spill. On this reduced-RAM host, the same RAM floor is
enforced immediately rather than waiting four samples; this tightens enforcement.
Each owned systemd scope retains `MemorySwapMax=0`; `MemoryMax` equals the
current OS-visible RAM, matching the earlier normal-host allowance rule.
The plugin's **8 GiB activation reserve is unchanged**. No other application,
DSH, gateway or maintenance schedule was stopped or changed.

## Attempts and results

1. The seven upstream CPU partition, dense math and quant-sharding test functions
   pass. Pytest is absent from the retained environment; its fixture-free test
   functions were called directly without installing dependencies.
2. The initial torchrun RCCL smoke attempt hit the RAM floor at **2.125 GiB**
   available. The unchanged control queued its first full warmup, then hit the
   same floor at **2.715 GiB**, during VAE loading. Neither completed its target.
3. A retry removed the extra torchrun launcher process. The two-peer smoke still
   stopped at **1.966 GiB** available. This is a RAM-blocked communication check,
   not evidence that gfx1100/RCCL transport is unsupported. The stock SP2 node
   separately fails registration in the isolated 0.42 server because
   `comfy.ldm.minimax.model.time_shift_slope` is absent.
4. After the user offered to close applications, the final attempt waited for
   additional RAM for up to ten minutes. The desired 10 GiB available target
   was not observed; the final check still found **7.599 GiB**.
   It then retried under the unchanged thresholds rather than treating the
   desired extra RAM as an existing admission rule. The isolated 0.42 pilot
   **registered successfully and its graph was accepted**. Both control and
   candidate stopped while loading the shared video VAE, before denoising or
   starting the SP2 rank-1 model worker. Thus these failures do not measure
   the memory/speed of a fully loaded SP2 DiT. Actual final outcomes follow.

## ComfyUI 0.42 compatibility pilot

The reviewed [Kaihui upstream](https://github.com/Kaihui-AMD/ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-MultiGPU/tree/ebf3953f6385360ef211d93e4ecf23696599eaa3)
remains at `ebf3953f6385360ef211d93e4ecf23696599eaa3`; its documented validation
targets ComfyUI 0.30 and R9700 cards. Inspection of the
[current H3 model](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/8f04fd6ecf4a548937971e840af2a985529fab76/comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py)
shows a changed layout constructor and final-layer signature; carried-audio
schedule conversion now belongs to outer `forward()`. A missing-helper alias
alone would not establish correct audio behavior.

The isolated [experimental patch](experiments/kaihui-h3-sp2-comfy042.patch)
removes the retired layout argument, supplies the new final-layer parameters,
returns native audio velocity for the current outer conversion, and fails
closed for reference/keyframe or masked-denoising paths. It does not change
SP partitioning, quantization, transport or the activation reserve.
Six CPU float32 zero-block synthetic cases match the native packing/output
boundary exactly for both video and audio, including carried audio scale 4.
These checks do not exercise quantized weights, attention or distributed
generation. The [CPU boundary checker](experiments/check-h3-sp2-comfy042.py)
and [upstream licence](experiments/LICENSE.Kaihui-H3) accompany the patch.
The pilot is not installed or qualified for normal use.

## Decision and source attribution

Keep the saved current workflow. No local SP2 performance or quality improvement
is established without successful full generations, matched timings and decoded
video/audio checks. Restoring RAM alone would not make the unmodified 0.30
loader compatible with 0.42; conversely, a CPU-tested compatibility patch does
not establish enough RAM for the current host.

[J I's dual-XTX SP2 report](https://lcz.me/post/28082) remains firsthand external
evidence with an unspecified plugin/workflow. It is not a local result and does
not identify the tested Kaihui revision. [Bunsei's Radiance TP2](https://lcz.me/topic/2582)
is a separate RDNA4/model-format route. See the [method/source review](H3-MULTIGPU-RESEARCH-2026-10-27.md).
